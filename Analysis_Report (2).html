GSE120575 Single-Cell RNA-seq Analysis Report

Dataset: GSE120575 (Sade-Feldman et al., 2018 - Melanoma Immunotherapy)

Total Cells Processed: 16290

Total Genes Analyzed: 45884

1. Summary of Workflow

  • QC & Normalization: Filtered low-quality cells (min 200 genes) and unexpressed genes. Normalized to 10,000 counts/cell and log1p transformed. Selected top 2,000 highly variable genes.
  • Clustering & Embedding: PCA (30 PCs), 15-nearest neighbor graph, UMAP projection, and Leiden clustering at resolution 0.8 (10 distinct clusters).
  • Annotation: Validated lineages using marker expression patterns across T-cell subsets, NK cells, Myeloid/Macrophages, Dendritic cells, Plasma cells, and B cells.

2. Key Findings

  • B Cells (Cluster 9): Strongly enriched in treatment Responders (~69%), supporting the key role of tertiary lymphoid structures and humoral immunity in anti-PD-1 efficacy.
  • Myeloid / Macrophages (Cluster 7): Heavily skewed toward Non-responders (~88%), underscoring the immunosuppressive myeloid barrier in non-responsive tumors.
